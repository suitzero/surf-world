📏 Measured results — including the failures

  • Teacher wave speed: 12.70 m/s measured vs 12.53 m/s theory (+1.4%) on a flat 16 m tank.
  • Teacher reflection: boundary reflection coefficient R = 0.237, above the 0.20 target — missed. Two sponge fixes were reverted because they broke reef stability.
  • Neural one-step prediction: the U-Net beat persistence by 13.9% on conditions it trained on (MSE 0.0289 vs 0.0336 m²) — but was 8.4% worse than persistence on swell it had never seen (0.0374 vs 0.0345 m²). One-step generalization failed. (Its 4 s rollout still beat persistence by 75%.)
  • Anytime curve (128×96): the teacher wins at every measured budget. The U-Net costs ~2.9× more per simulated second than the teacher at this scale — there is no budget here where the neural model pays off.
  • Surfer AI: the forecast agent tied the rule agent (642 vs 646 catches); the multi-step planner lost (615 vs 646) and its search-budget curve never rose. Prediction did not raise catch rates with the current forecast field. The planner was safer: 20% fewer wipeouts, zero drop-ins.
  • Hybrid corrections: the corrected breaking-onset line matches the teacher's own labels at a median of 0 cells (fast path alone: 1–3 cells off). Stale corrections are dropped by the state-version rule — demonstrated and counted, never applied.
  • Partitioned solver: bit-exact against the monolithic run at 2 and 4 partitions; halo exchange cost 2.7% at 4 partitions. Distribution has no benefit at game scale on one machine.

Every number above comes from this project's own measurement runs (project notes S07–S17). Failures are listed on purpose — that is the point of measuring.
